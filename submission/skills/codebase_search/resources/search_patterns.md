# Search Patterns & Best Practices

1. **Ignore Dirs**: Searches automatically skip `.git`, `target`, `build`, `node_modules`, `.idea`, `venv`.
2. **File Types**: Narrow searches with `--file-types .java,.kt` for fast indexing.
3. **Regex Search**: Use `--regex` for symbols: `class\s+\w+Controller` or `def\s+[a-z_]+`.
